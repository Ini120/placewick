Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us — especially because Fledgely handles personal documents such as CVs, transcripts, and placement letters. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Fledgely, we may collect:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and the role you sign up as (student or company).
  • Profile information — for students: your school, department, course, level, skills, and links you choose to add. For companies: company name, industry, website, and description.
  • Documents you upload — such as your CV, cover letter, SIWES letter, IT placement letter, reference letter, transcript, or certificates.
  • Application data — the internships you apply to, documents you attach to an application, and messages you send through the platform.
  • Waitlist details — if you join our waitlist, your name, email, department, and school.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and operate your account.
  • To match students with relevant internships and help companies review applicants.
  • To let you apply to roles and attach your documents.
  • To enable messaging between students and companies.
  • To contact you about your account or about Fledgely's launch and updates.

3. How we store and protect your information

Your information is stored securely with our hosting and database providers. Documents you upload are kept private to you. They are only shared with a company when you choose to attach them to an application for that company's role — never browsable by companies otherwise.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With companies you apply to — limited to the application and any documents you attach.
  • With service providers who help us run Fledgely (such as hosting and database providers), under appropriate safeguards.
  • Where required by law.

5. Your rights

You can access and update your information at any time in your profile settings. You may request deletion of your account and uploaded documents through your account settings or by contacting us. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable Nigerian data-protection law.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your profile and uploaded documents from the platform.

7. Children

Fledgely is intended for users aged 16 and above. If you are under 16, please do not use the platform.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Fledgely grows. We will post the updated version here with a new "last updated" date.

Terms of Service

By creating an account or using Fledgely, you agree to these terms.

1. What Fledgely is

Fledgely is a platform that connects students with internship and early-career opportunities posted by companies and organisations. We provide the tools to discover roles, apply, and communicate — we are not an employer and we are not a party to any internship arrangement.

2. No guarantee of placement

Submitting an application through Fledgely does not guarantee an internship, interview, or offer. Hiring decisions are made entirely by the companies and organisations, not by Fledgely.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, truthful information and documents.
  • Keep your login details secure and do not share your account.
  • Use Fledgely only for lawful, genuine internship and recruitment purposes.
  • Do not upload documents that are fraudulent, forged, or belong to someone else.
  • Do not harass, spam, or mislead other users.

4. Rules for companies and organisations

  • Post only genuine internship and early-career opportunities.
  • Do not request payment from students as a condition of applying or being hired.
  • Use applicants' information and documents only to evaluate them for the role they applied to.
  • Treat applicants fairly and professionally.

5. Content and accounts

You keep ownership of the documents and information you upload. By uploading, you give Fledgely permission to store and display that content as needed to run the service (for example, showing your attached documents to a company you apply to). We may suspend or remove accounts that break these terms.

6. Availability

We work to keep Fledgely running smoothly but cannot guarantee uninterrupted access. Features may change as the platform develops.

Disclaimer

Fledgely does not guarantee the accuracy of information posted by companies or organisations, and is not responsible for the conduct of any user or for hiring decisions. We encourage students to verify opportunities independently and to be cautious of any request for payment. Use of Fledgely is at your own discretion and risk.

Contact
