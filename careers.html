Help us make placements work for Nigeria

Placewick Technologies Limited is building technology for the way Nigerian students, companies and institutions connect. We are an early-stage team and we are interested in meeting talented people.

Open roles

We do not have any open roles at the moment. We will list them here when we do.

If you would like to work with us, send your CV and a short note about what you would like to do to hello@placewick.com. We read every message and will keep your details for when a suitable role opens.

Areas we expect to grow

Engineering

Building and maintaining the web platform, from the student experience to institution tools.

Product and design

Shaping simple, clear experiences for people who use Placewick every day.

Partnerships

Working with institutions and companies to bring them onto the platform.

Community and support

Helping students, companies and coordinators get the most from Placewick.

How we like to work

We solve real problems

Every feature starts from something students, companies or coordinators struggle with today.

We keep things clear

Plain language, simple screens and honest communication, inside the team and with users.

We build trust first

Verification and privacy are part of the product, not an afterthought.
