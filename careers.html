Why work with us

A real problem

Thousands of students and schools deal with SIWES placement every year. What you build gets used.

Early-stage ownership

A small team means you shape the product and the company from the start.

Built for Nigeria

We design around how Nigerian institutions and companies actually work.

Learn quickly

You will work across the product and learn from the people around you.

How we hire

  1. 1

    Tell us about yourself

    Send a short note and your CV or portfolio using the form below.

  2. 2

    A conversation

    If there is a fit, we will have an informal chat about you and what we are building.

  3. 3

    A practical exercise

    For some roles, a short task related to the work, so we can see how you think.

  4. 4

    An offer

    We aim to give a clear decision and be honest about the stage we are at.
