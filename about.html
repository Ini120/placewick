THE PROBLEM

Finding opportunities shouldn't feel impossible.

For many Nigerian students, getting practical experience can be difficult. Opportunities can be scattered across different platforms, social media pages, personal connections, and word of mouth.

Opportunities are scattered

Students often have to search through multiple platforms just to find internships, SIWES placements, volunteering opportunities, and entry-level roles.

It's difficult to know what's genuine

Students need a more reliable way to discover opportunities and identify companies they can confidently apply to.

Students need better connections

Talent exists everywhere, but students don't always have direct access to companies looking for emerging talent.

WHY PLACEWICK EXISTS

We're making the path from student to career easier.

Placewick was created around a simple idea: students shouldn't have to rely only on who they know to discover opportunities.

We want to create a space where students can discover opportunities that match their interests and skills, while companies can find motivated young talent.

WHAT DRIVES US

Built with a purpose.

OUR MISSION

Make meaningful opportunities more accessible to Nigerian students.

We aim to simplify the process of discovering, applying for, and connecting with opportunities that help students gain experience and grow.

OUR VISION

A future where every student can find their opportunity.

We envision a connected ecosystem where students and companies can find each other based on skills, interests, and potential.

HOW WE HELP

One platform. Two sides.

Placewick is designed to create value for both students looking for opportunities and companies looking for talent.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Built from a desire to make things better.

Placewick started with a simple observation: finding opportunities shouldn't depend entirely on connections, luck, or knowing where to look.

As a student, I understand how difficult it can be to navigate internships, SIWES, career opportunities, and the transition from school into the working world.

Placewick is my attempt to build something that makes that journey easier for students while also helping companies discover the talent that already exists.

Inioluwa Aiyenuyo Founder, Placewick
