About Placewick

Making SIWES placement simple, visible and verified

Every year, thousands of Nigerian students struggle to find a place for their industrial training, and their institutions struggle to keep track of where everyone ended up. Placewick brings students, companies and institutions onto one platform so that placements are easier to find, easier to manage and easier to prove.

The problem we are solving

Finding a SIWES placement often depends on who you know. Students move between offices with printed letters, logbooks travel across states in physical booklets, and coordinators chase updates by phone. Companies that are willing to host students have no simple way to reach them, and schools have little data to show for accreditation.

What changes with Placewick

  • Students find and apply for placements in one place
  • Companies review applicants and confirm placements
  • Logbooks and forms are completed and signed digitally
  • Schools see placements and reports in real time

How it works

  1. 1

    Register

    Students, companies and institutions create accounts. Companies and institutions are verified before they are activated.

  2. 2

    Match and apply

    Students browse verified opportunities and apply with a reusable profile and documents.

  3. 3

    Get placed

    Companies review applicants, interview and confirm placements. The student's institution sees the update.

  4. 4

    Complete and report

    Weekly logbooks, Form 8 and evaluations are signed digitally, supervisors are assigned, and reports are ready for accreditation.

What we stand for

Trust

We verify companies and institutions so students are placed with real organisations.

Access

A good placement should not depend on who you know.

Clarity

Everyone in the process can see where a placement stands.

Privacy

Student information is shared only with the people who need it.
