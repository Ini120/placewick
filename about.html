About Placewick

Making SIWES placement simple, visible and verified

Every year, thousands of Nigerian students struggle to find a place for their industrial training, and their institutions struggle to keep track of where everyone ended up. Placewick brings students, companies and institutions onto one platform so that placements are easier to find, easier to manage and easier to prove.

Verified companies Digital logbooks Accreditation-ready reports
Our mission

Make good placements available to every student

A student's access to practical experience should not depend on who they know. We build tools that open the door, and keep it open from application to final report.

Our vision

A trusted record of every placement in Nigeria

Students, companies and institutions all working from one clear, verified record, so that nothing is lost between a placement letter and a signed logbook.

The problem we are solving

Finding and managing a SIWES placement is still mostly manual. Here is what that looks like today, and what changes.

Today

  • Placements depend on personal contacts
  • Printed letters and trips between offices
  • Physical logbooks carried across states
  • Coordinators chasing updates by phone
  • Little data to show for accreditation

With Placewick

  • Students browse and apply to verified roles
  • Acceptance and Form 8 handled digitally
  • Weekly logbooks signed online
  • Placements visible to the school in real time
  • Reports ready for NUC and NBTE audits

Where we are going

We are building in the open and in stages. This is our direction, and it may change as we learn from students, companies and schools.

  1. Now

    SIWES-first platform

    Student, company and institution tools, with verification and digital logbooks, working as prototypes.

  2. Next

    Going live

    Real accounts, secure data storage and messaging, then onboarding our first partner institutions and companies.

  3. Later

    Beyond SIWES

    Graduate placement and more digital services from Placewick Technologies Limited.
