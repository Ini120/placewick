Guides to help you through your placement

Practical, plain-language guidance for students on finding a placement, preparing for it and getting the most from it.

For students

What is SIWES?

SIWES, the Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme, is a programme that gives students in tertiary institutions practical experience in their field before they graduate. It is coordinated by the Industrial Training Fund (ITF) together with institutions and regulators such as the NUC, NBTE and NCCE. How long your training lasts, and when you take it, depends on your programme and your institution, so confirm the details with your SIWES coordinator.

How to choose a good placement

Look for a company whose work matches your course, so that what you do counts as relevant experience. Ask who will supervise you, what tasks you will handle, and whether the company can sign your logbook and evaluation. Be careful with any placement that asks you to pay a fee to be accepted. On Placewick, companies are verified before they can post.

Writing a CV for a placement

Keep it to one or two pages. Start with your name, contact details, institution, course and level. Add a short profile of two or three lines, then your skills, projects, volunteer work or any previous experience. Use clear headings and plain language, save it as a PDF, and name the file with your name, for example Ada-Obi-CV.pdf.

Preparing for an interview

Read about the company and the role beforehand. Prepare short answers to common questions such as why you want the placement, what you have learned in your course, and a project you are proud of. Arrive on time, dress neatly, and ask at least one question about the work you would do.

Keeping a useful logbook

Write in your logbook every week, not in bulk at the end. Record what you did, what you learned and any problems you solved, in your own words and with enough detail to be specific. Ask your supervisor to review and sign each entry promptly. A complete, signed logbook is part of how your placement is assessed.

Making the most of your placement

Be punctual, ask questions and take notes. Offer to help with tasks beyond the minimum, and keep a record of what you achieve. Near the end, ask your supervisor for feedback and, if appropriate, a reference. Stay in touch with the people you worked with.

For companies and institutions

Hosting students well

Give each student a named supervisor, a clear set of tasks and regular feedback. Sign logbooks every week and complete evaluations on time. Students learn more, and your team benefits from their work.

Running SIWES for your school

Keep a single record of where every student is placed, assign supervisors by location, and keep your logbook and form records in one place so reports for accreditation are ready when needed.
