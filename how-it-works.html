HOW PLACEWICK WORKS

From searching to getting connected.

Placewick connects students with meaningful opportunities while helping organizations discover emerging Nigerian talent through a trusted platform.

FOR STUDENTS

Find opportunities that move you forward.

Create your profile, discover opportunities that match your goals, apply, and connect with organizations looking for emerging talent.

01

Create your profile

Build your Placewick profile with information about your education, skills, interests, experience, and career goals.

02

Discover opportunities

Browse internships, SIWES placements, graduate opportunities, volunteering roles, and other opportunities relevant to your career journey.

03

Apply

Review opportunity requirements and submit your application through the process provided by the organization.

04

Connect and grow

Connect with organizations, gain practical experience, develop your skills, and take the next step in your career.

FOR COMPANIES

Build trust. Find talent. Create opportunities.

Placewick helps organizations establish a trusted presence, publish opportunities, and connect with students and graduates.

01

Register your company

Create an organization account and provide the required company information so Placewick can establish your organization's identity.

02

Submit verification details

Provide the required company registration information, including relevant CAC details and supporting information needed for verification.

03

Verification review

Placewick reviews the submitted company information and verifies that the organization meets the platform's requirements.

04

Get verified

Once approved, the organization receives a verified company status and can use the platform to create and manage opportunities.

05

Post opportunities

Create internship, SIWES, graduate, volunteering, and other opportunity listings with the details students need to apply.

06

Review applicants

Receive applications from students and graduates and review candidates based on the requirements of your opportunity.

07

Connect with candidates

Communicate with suitable applicants and continue your organization's selection process.

08

Find emerging talent

Build connections with students and graduates while creating meaningful early-career opportunities.

TRUST & VERIFICATION

Helping students discover opportunities with confidence.

Students need to know that the organizations they interact with are genuine. That's why Placewick includes a company verification process before organizations can operate fully on the platform.

Company information and relevant registration details are submitted for review before the organization receives verified status.

Company information

Organizations provide the required business information during registration.

CAC details

Relevant Corporate Affairs Commission registration information is submitted where required.

Information review

Submitted information is reviewed as part of the company's verification process.

Verified organization

Approved organizations can receive verified status and proceed with platform activities.
