How it works

One platform for the whole placement journey

Whether you are a student looking for a placement, a company ready to host one, or an institution keeping track, here is how Placewick works for you.

Find a placement and track it to the end

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up as a student, choose your institution and department, and add your matriculation number.

  2. 2

    Build your profile

    Add your skills, interests and documents such as your CV and admission letter once, and reuse them for every application.

  3. 3

    Browse and apply

    Search verified SIWES and internship opportunities, save the ones you like, and apply in a few clicks.

  4. 4

    Track and communicate

    Follow each application from applied to interview to placed, and message companies directly.

  5. 5

    Complete your placement

    Fill in your weekly logbook online. Your workplace supervisor signs it digitally, and your school can see your progress.

Reach students and manage placements in one place

  1. 1

    Register your company

    Create an account and submit your company details. Our team verifies each company before it goes live.

  2. 2

    Post opportunities

    Publish internship and SIWES roles with the skills, location and duration you need.

  3. 3

    Review applicants

    See ranked applicants, shortlist, schedule interviews and message candidates.

  4. 4

    Confirm placements

    Accept a student and the placement is recorded, with the acceptance form ready to be signed digitally.

  5. 5

    Supervise and sign off

    Sign weekly logbooks and submit evaluations online, with no booklets to carry around.

Common questions

Is Placewick free for students?

Creating a student account and applying for opportunities is free. We will announce any future paid features clearly before they are introduced.

How do you verify companies and institutions?

Each company and institution submits its details when it registers. Our team reviews them before the account is activated, so students apply to real organisations and schools see real data.

Can I use Placewick if I am not on SIWES?

Placewick currently focuses on SIWES and internship placements for students of Nigerian tertiary institutions. Graduate placement may be added later.

Who can see my information?

Your institution can see your placement progress, and a company can see your profile when you apply to it. Your information is not shared with anyone else.

What happens to my logbook?

You fill it in weekly on Placewick. Your workplace supervisor signs it online and your institution reviews it, so it is all kept in one place.

My school is not on Placewick yet. What can I do?

Ask your SIWES coordinator to register the institution, or send us the school's name at partnerships@placewick.com and we will reach out.

How do I report a problem or a suspicious company?

Write to support@placewick.com with the details. We review every report.
